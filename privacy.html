Desktop Flow Privacy Policy

Updated: November 4, 2026. Applies to the first Mac App Store edition.

Desktop Flow works locally on your Mac. It does not transmit information, collect analytics, display advertising, track you or require an account.

After you select your Desktop folder, the app reads item names, file identifiers, file metadata and icon positions to distinguish new items from renamed or manually positioned ones. It observes changes to the folder. It does not read file contents, rename your files or move them to another folder. Finder performs icon position changes after you enable custom placement and grant macOS Automation permission.

The app also reads Finder's Desktop grouping preference locally to check whether Stacks are enabled. It does not change Finder preferences. An unreadable or unrecognised state pauses positioning. This preference is not transmitted.

Settings and a security-scoped bookmark for your selected folder are stored in the app's local container. A bounded local troubleshooting log contains counts, coordinates, timestamps and error decisions. It does not contain filenames or file contents and is not uploaded. The app does not share data with third parties.

You can stop positioning at any time with the Custom placement switch or by quitting the app. You can revoke Finder Automation and folder access in macOS System Settings under Privacy & Security. Removing the application may leave its local container on the Mac; no remote account or server-side data exists.

Questions: phlo@phlo.pl. If you email support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Do not include private files, passwords or verification codes.
